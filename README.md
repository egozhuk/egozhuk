## About me

I’m a software engineer focused on building products that are not just functional, but clean, fast, and pleasant to use.

I enjoy working at the intersection of engineering, design, and product thinking:
turning rough ideas into polished experiences, automating repetitive work, and shipping solutions end-to-end.

What matters to me most:
- clear architecture
- thoughtful UX
- maintainable code
- practical automation
- real product value

I like systems that feel simple on the surface and solid underneath.
